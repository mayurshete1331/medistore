How Medi Automated Procurement Works

1

Continuous Monitoring

System tracks sales in real-time from the POS. When remaining stock ≤ reorder threshold, an alert is triggered automatically.

2

Suggested vs Custom Qty

System computes default safety replenishment, but allows the owner/manager to customize exact pack quantities as needed.

3

Owner Authorization Gate

No distributor is messaged without explicit authorization. The store owner approves orders with a single click.

4

Instant WhatsApp & Email Dispatch

1-Click generates pre-formatted, legal Purchase Orders complete with DL number, packaging spec, and direct distributor chat.
